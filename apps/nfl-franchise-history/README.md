# NFL Franchise History

Every NFL club's story on a felt pennant: where it came from, what it has won,
the rival it measures itself against and the moments its fans still talk
about. No key, no account and no network. All the history is built into the
app. Built for scroll panels (192x32).

## Settings

| setting | what it is |
|---|---|
| **Team** | `ALL TEAMS` (default) moves through all 32 clubs, a new one every 10 minutes. Pick a club to see only its pennant and its stories. |

## What the pages show

- **Legacy**: the club's pennant. A felt pennant in the club's colours flies
  from a wooden stick across the panel. The nickname is stitched along it in
  one size of letter, the biggest that fits the narrowing cloth. Above the tail are the home market, the founding year
  (`EST 1960`) and the year of the club's last title (`TITLE 1965`, or
  `NO TITLE` for the five clubs that have never won one). Under the tail
  stand the trophies: a silver Lombardi Trophy for each Super Bowl win and a
  dim ghost Lombardi for each Super Bowl lost, with the record (`SB 0-4`)
  beside them. New England's twelve trips do not fit, so it shows its six
  wins and the record reads `SB 6-6`. Clubs that have never played in a Super
  Bowl say so in that spot. A bronze cup and a count such as `+9 NFL` or
  `+2 AFL` show the league championships the club won outside a Super Bowl:
  NFL titles before the Super Bowl era, AFL titles, and 1966-69 league titles
  followed by a Super Bowl loss. AAFC titles are not counted, because the NFL
  does not count them. Super Bowl results run through Super Bowl LX
  (February 2026).
- **Trivia**: a new frame each minute, with a row of small squares at the top
  right showing which one is up.
  - **Stories**: one true story from the club's history, such as how it got
    its name, a famous play, a record or an oddity. The year it happened is
    stitched on a short club pennant beside the story. There are 198
    stories, six or seven per club, and each fits three lines of text.
  - **Rivalry**: the club's pennant (its abbreviation stitched on) facing its
    rival's logo, with the all-time series between them (wins-losses-ties,
    playoffs included, through the 2025 season) and who leads it. Washington
    has no rivalry frame, because each of its rivalries had already been
    played in 2026.
  - **Retired number**: a pixel-art jersey in the club's colours with the
    number of one of its most famous retired players, and his name. Dallas and
    the Raiders do not retire numbers, and Atlanta, Baltimore, Houston and New
    Orleans have no entry here, so those six clubs skip this frame.

Years are the year the game was played, so a Super Bowl counts in the
February it was won (Seattle's Super Bowl LX is 2026).

Refresh is 60 seconds because the trivia page turns over every minute.
