# NFL Franchise History

Every NFL club's story beside its logo: where it came from, what it has won,
the rival it measures itself against and the moments its fans still talk
about. No key, no account and no network. All the history is built into the
app. Built for scroll panels (192x32).

## Settings

| setting | what it is |
|---|---|
| **Team** | `ALL TEAMS` (default) moves through all 32 clubs, a new one every 10 minutes. Pick a club to see only its trophy shelf and its stories. |

## What the pages show

- **Legacy**: the club's logo, then its nickname in the club colour over a
  two-tone club stripe, the home market and the founding year (`EST 1960`).
  On the right is a trophy shelf: a silver Lombardi Trophy for each Super
  Bowl win and a dim ghost Lombardi for each Super Bowl lost, so New
  England's twelve trips show as six of each. A bronze cup with a count
  stands beside them for the league championships the club won outside a
  Super Bowl: NFL titles before the Super Bowl era, AFL titles, and 1966-69
  league titles followed by a Super Bowl loss. AAFC titles are not counted,
  because the NFL does not count them. Under the shelf are the Super Bowl
  record (`SB 0-4`) and the year of the last title (`LAST TITLE 1965`, or
  `NO TITLE YET` for the five clubs that have never won one). A club with no
  trophies at all has an empty shelf. Super Bowl results run through Super
  Bowl LX (February 2026).
- **Trivia**: a new frame each minute, with a row of small squares at the top
  right showing which one is up.
  - **Stories**: one true story from the club's history, such as how it got
    its name, a famous play, a record or an oddity. The club logo sits beside the
    story, with the year it happened above it. There are 198
    stories, six or seven per club, and each fits three lines of text.
  - **Rivalry**: the club's logo facing its rival's logo, with the all-time series between them (wins-losses-ties,
    playoffs included, through the 2025 season) and who leads it. Washington
    has no rivalry frame, because each of its rivalries had already been
    played in 2026.
  - **Retired number**: a pixel-art jersey in the club's colours with the
    number of one of its most famous retired players, and his name. Dallas and
    the Raiders do not retire numbers, and Atlanta, Baltimore, Houston and New
    Orleans have no entry here, so those six clubs skip this frame.

Years are the year the game was played, so a Super Bowl counts in the
February it was won (Seattle's Super Bowl LX is 2026).

Refresh is 60 seconds because the trivia page turns over every minute.
