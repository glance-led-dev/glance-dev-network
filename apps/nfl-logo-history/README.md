# NFL Logo History

Every primary logo an NFL club has worn, oldest to newest, on a timeline with
the season each one debuted, flowing into today's logo. Pick a club, or leave
it on **ALL TEAMS** to rotate through the league (a new club every 3 minutes).

- **Timeline:** up to five older logos per frame above a timeline, then
  today's logo, larger and between gold brackets. For a club that moved, the
  timeline is its road map: each stretch carries the city its logos debuted in
  (CHICAGO, ST. LOUIS, then the Cardinals' Arizona logo). Clubs with more
  history step through frames once a minute (the dots beside the gold year
  show which frame). Clubs with little history use the free space for their
  name and the logo count.
- **First, longest, now:** the club's first logo and today's logo either side
  of its longest-serving logo and the number of seasons it was worn (the
  Cowboys' star: 63 seasons since 1964).

No network and no key: everything ships in the app.

## Which logos count

**Primary logos only, and a colour tweak is not a new logo.** An update that
only adjusts colour shades (a darker orange, a thinner outline, a new blue)
is merged into the logo it updates. A new drawing, or a part of the logo
recoloured so it looks different (the Browns' facemask, the Rams' horn),
starts a new era. Each logo is dated by the season it debuted, and its city
is the city it debuted in. The timeline states the rule in its free space
("RECOLORS MERGED").

So the Cowboys' star dates from 1964 (the 2002 update was a colour spec), the
Colts' horseshoe from 1961, the Steelmark from 1962, the Raiders' shield from
1964, the Texans' bull from 2002, and the Bears have five logos, not seven
(the navy bear head was an alternate, never the primary).

## Where the logos come from

- **Eras and years:** each club's logo history, checked against Wikipedia's
  "Logos and uniforms" articles, the clubs' own sites (Bengals media guide,
  Broncos, Packers, Patriots, Rams, Steelers museum), NFL.com and Uni Watch.
  SportsLogos.Net supplied most of the historic artwork.
- **Today's logos:** the hand-tuned 40 x 24 set from Fantasy Football News,
  except the Rams (2026 solid-colour LA monogram) and Titans (2026 Shield),
  which are new 40 x 24 conversions of the 2026 marks.
- **Older logos:** converted from the full-size historic marks at 26 x 19 (a
  wordmark gets a double-width cell) and scored against those originals; every
  one scores 90+ for craft and faithfulness. Where a whole mark can't read at
  that size, the tile shows its main element (the Oilers' 1960 oilman, the
  Bengals' 1968 tiger).
- **Washington, 1932-2019:** the club's marks from those years used Native
  American imagery that it has since retired. They appear as one plain tile
  ("9 MARKS", with the year), not as pictures. The 2020-21 Washington Football
  Team wordmark doesn't read at 26 px, so it is shown as a "WFT" tile.
