# College Football Program History

The trophy case and history of 39 major FBS programs, on a 192 x 32 panel. No network, no key.

- **legacy**: the school's logo on a nameplate with its conference (SEC, BIG TEN, ACC, BIG 12, PAC-12, AMERICAN, INDEP.), its name and mascot, then the trophy case. That's claimed national titles beside a gold trophy and Heisman winners beside a bronze stiff-arm figure. Under it are the home stadium and the first season.
- **tradition**: one story from the program's history a minute (190+ in all): players, coaches, titles, trophies, rivalries and streaks. When a story names another program, that program's logo faces off against the school's own (the Iron Bowl shows Auburn, the 2024 title shows Notre Dame). Other stories get a pixel-art icon.

Stadium and gameday lore (the 12th Man, Jump Around, the Grove) is in **College Stadium Gameday**. Most Heisman stories are in **Heisman Trophy History**. This app tells each school's Heisman story once, so the three apps can share a wall without repeating each other.

## Setting

**Program**: one school, or `ALL - ROTATE` to cycle through all 39, a new school every 10 minutes.

## Notes on the numbers

- National titles are each program's own **claimed** count. College football crowned champions by poll for a century, so counts differ by source.
- Heisman counts are awards, through the 2025 season. Archie Griffin counts twice, and Reggie Bush's 2005 award counts because it was reinstated in 2024.
- Conferences are the 2026 line-up: Boise State is in the rebuilt Pac-12, and Army and Navy are in the American.
- Texas A&M's logo ships as `TAMU.png`.
