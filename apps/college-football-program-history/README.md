# College Football Program History

The trophy case and history of 39 major FBS programs, on a 192 x 32 panel. No network, no key.

- **legacy**: a trophy shelf across the whole panel. Every claimed national title is a gold cup standing on it and every Heisman winner is a bronze stiff-arm statue, so Alabama's 18 titles are 18 cups (counted in fives). The shelf's walnut lip carries the counts ("18 CLAIMED TITLES", "4 HEISMANS"). The school and mascot are on the wall above, with the conference (SEC, BIG TEN, ACC, BIG 12, PAC-12, AMERICAN, INDEP.), and the school's felt banner hangs at the right with its logo and first season. A program with no titles or no Heismans shows a grey ghost in the empty spot.
- **tradition**: the same wall with a framed plaque. It shows one story from the program's history a minute (190+ in all): players, coaches, titles, trophies, rivalries and streaks. When a story names another program, that program's logo goes on the plaque, facing the school's banner (the Iron Bowl shows Auburn, the 2024 title shows Notre Dame). Other stories get a pixel-art icon.

Stadium and gameday lore (the 12th Man, Jump Around, the Grove) is in **College Stadium Gameday**. Most Heisman stories are in **Heisman Trophy History**. This app tells each school's Heisman story once, so the three apps can share a wall without repeating each other.

## Setting

**Program**: one school, or `ALL - ROTATE` to cycle through all 39, a new school every 10 minutes.

## Notes on the numbers

- National titles are each program's own **claimed** count. College football crowned champions by poll for a century, so counts differ by source.
- Heisman counts are awards, through the 2025 season. Archie Griffin counts twice, and Reggie Bush's 2005 award counts because it was reinstated in 2024.
- Conferences are the 2026 line-up: Boise State is in the rebuilt Pac-12, and Army and Navy are in the American.
- Texas A&M's logo ships as `TAMU.png` (40 x 24, the plaque's face-off) and `S_TAMU.png` (24 x 18, the banner). The banner uses the authored 24 x 18 set, never a scaled copy, on a cream or near-black window picked per logo for contrast.
- The home stadium is no longer drawn: the shelf needs the room. **College Stadium Gameday** covers stadiums.
