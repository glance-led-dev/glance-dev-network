# Carrier artwork

The bundled 41x28 PNG tiles are scaled from these carrier-hosted assets, retrieved September 26, 2026:

- UPS: https://www.ups.com/assets/resources/webcontent/images/ups-logo.svg
- FedEx: https://www.fedex.com/content/dam/fedex-com/logos/FedEx-Logo.png
- USPS: https://www.usps.com/global-elements/images/logo_mobile.svg

The original logo proportions and colors are retained. These marks belong to their respective owners; this app is not affiliated with or endorsed by the carriers.
