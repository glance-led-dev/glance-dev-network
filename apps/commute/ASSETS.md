# Starbucks artwork

`assets/starbucks.png` is a 24-pixel rendering of the Starbucks siren logo, sourced from https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/d/d3/Starbucks_Corporation_Logo_2011.svg . The Starbucks name and logo belong to Starbucks; inclusion identifies the configured stop and does not imply affiliation. The iced-coffee cup is original native-pixel drawing code.

The branded screen is selected when the configured stop name contains Starbucks. Other stop names retain the general-purpose route screen.
