# Thanksgiving Countdown

Thanksgiving Countdown shows the days until US Thanksgiving (the fourth Thursday of November) in your choice of five designs. On the day it says HAPPY THANKSGIVING, then at midnight it starts counting toward next year.

## Configuration

- **Design** — Thanksgiving table, Turkey and fall tree, Fall scene, Cornucopia, or Harvest moon.
- **Time zone** — Eastern, Central, Mountain, Arizona, Pacific, Alaska, or Hawaii. Sets when the count changes, so Thanksgiving starts and ends at your own midnight.
