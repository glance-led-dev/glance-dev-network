# Weather Report

Estimated conditions and forecast for any US ZIP on a 192x32 display. No weather station or key needed.

**Pages**

- **Now:** a color block for the sky, the temperature, today's high and low, the city, and what's next, like `RAIN AROUND 4 PM`, `RAIN ENDING ABOUT 11 PM`, `FREEZING TONIGHT` or `DRY NEXT 24 HRS`. `FEELS` appears when the heat index or wind chill is 3 degrees or more from the temperature.
- **Air:** humidity, dew point, pressure and its trend, UV and air quality.
- **Wind:** speed, direction and gusts. The arrow points the way the wind is blowing.
- **Rain:** totals for today, 7 days and 30 days, and the highest hourly chance in the next 24 hours.
- **Today:** the day's temperature, a solid line so far and dotted ahead.
- **Next hours:** the next 8 hours.
- **Forecast:** 5 days. Each bar runs from the low to the high, all on one scale. After sunset, `TODAY` becomes `TONIGHT`.
- **Sky:** sunrise, sunset, daylight and the moon.

**Alerts:** a warning takes over the Now page in its NWS color. A watch or advisory runs along the bottom of it.

Numbers are model estimates for the center of the ZIP, not readings from a station.

Data: [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com), [Zippopotam](https://zippopotam.us), [NWS](https://weather.gov) and [USNO](https://aa.usno.navy.mil). No key required.
