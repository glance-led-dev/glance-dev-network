# Team logo artwork

The bundled PNGs come from the public ESPN team logo CDN, using the dark-background variant where available. The app renders them at 16 × 16 with the GDN nearest-neighbor renderer; these are pixel-rendered team marks, not newly hand-drawn illustrations. Team names and marks belong to their respective teams. ESPN’s image service supplied the compact 64 × 64 PNGs required by Glance’s asset limit. Source retrieval: 2026-09-13.

Coverage: all 32 NFL teams; 67 football teams in the ACC, Big Ten, Big 12 and SEC; Oregon State, Washington State and Notre Dame. This includes every school in the historic Power Five and the newer power-conference additions.
