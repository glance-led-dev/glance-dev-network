# NC Election Results

Live North Carolina election results from the State Board of Elections, for a 192x32 display.

**County** sets the view:

- **All NC:** the statewide races and all 14 US House districts.
- **One county:** that county's own ballot, down to commissioners, school board and local referendums. Races that reach past the county add a dim third column: the statewide (NC) or district-wide (DIST) share of the same race.

**Show** narrows the list: All races, County races, Ballot questions or Judges.

**Pages**

- **Card:** a countdown, then polling hours, then precincts in, ballots cast, turnout and the time of the last update.
- **7 race pages:** the top race always holds the first page. On a longer ballot the other six move on at each refresh, and the header counts through the ballot (`3/21`). `4/11 IN` means 4 of 11 precincts are in.

With County on All NC and Show on All races, two pages follow the US Senate on every pass:

- **NC Legislature:** seats each party is leading in the NC House and Senate, with gold lines at the three-fifths mark (72 of 120, 30 of 50). The darker part of each bar is unopposed seats. These are leads, not results.
- **Closest Races:** the three tightest US and state races by votes ahead, once a quarter of a race's precincts are in.

Each race lists its candidates leader first. Up to four show votes and share; five to eight show in two columns with share only; past eight, the last spot says how many more there are (`+2 MORE`). `PICK 3` means voters choose three. `D`, `R`, `L` and `G` mark the party. In the All NC view, `(i)` marks a sitting officeholder; the state's data doesn't carry this, so it's a checked list in the app, and county views don't show it. A write-in line appears once it has votes. One-name races are left out.

**Nothing is called.** The state's feed has no winner field, and election night totals leave out late absentee and provisional ballots, so all counts are unofficial.

Early votes usually post about 8:00 to 8:30 PM ET, and Election Day precincts follow until about 1 AM.

Data: [er.ncsbe.gov](https://er.ncsbe.gov), no key required.
