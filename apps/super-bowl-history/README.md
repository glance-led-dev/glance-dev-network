# Super Bowl History

Every Super Bowl from I to LX, each franchise's trophy case, the MVP leaders and a countdown to the next Super Bowl. The data is built into the app, so it needs no key and no network.

## Pages

- **game**: one Super Bowl at a time.
  - The champion's logo is on the left, with confetti in its colours, and the runner-up's on the right.
  - The final score sits either side of a pixel-art Lombardi Trophy, with the winner's score in gold. Overtime games are marked OT.
  - The bottom line changes each minute:
    - the MVP, beside a medal (two medals for Super Bowl XII's co-MVPs);
    - the full date and host city, with a calendar and a map pin;
    - for a relocated club, the name it played under that day (WON AS THE OAKLAND RAIDERS, OVER THE BALTIMORE COLTS);
    - for the famous games, a one-line story beside a star (WIDE RIGHT, WON BY 1 POINT; 1ST OT GAME, BACK FROM 28-3).
  - **On this day**: on a game's anniversary (US Eastern date), that game is pinned all day and its date line reads ON THIS DAY IN 1967. When several games share the date, they take turns.
- **rings**: one franchise's trophy case.
  - A trophy for every appearance, in date order, on a wooden shelf: silver for a win, a shadow for a loss.
  - Under each trophy is a plate with the year of the game: gold for a title, grey for a loss.
  - On the right: the number of titles, and OF n appearances.
  - Every other minute, a club with a record to its name shows it in amber in place of its name (4 STRAIGHT SUPER BOWLS, MOST SUPER BOWL TRIPS). The latest champion shows REIGNING CHAMPIONS until the next Super Bowl.
- **legends**: two screens, one a minute.
  - The countdown to Super Bowl LXI: days to go, then Feb 14 2027 at SoFi Stadium, Inglewood. On game day it reads TODAY. After the game this screen stops showing until the data is updated.
  - The MVP board: every multiple MVP winner, with one medal per award (Brady's five in a row). With a club picked, that club's MVPs under its logo, with medal ribbons in its colours.

## Settings

- **Team**:
  - **ALL TEAMS** shows every Super Bowl in turn, one screen a minute, every franchise's trophy case (most titles first), and the all-time MVP leaders.
  - Pick a club to see only its Super Bowls, its own trophy case and its own MVPs. The four clubs that have never appeared (Browns, Lions, Jaguars, Texans) get a "no appearances yet" screen.

## Notes

- Relocated clubs use today's logo: the Baltimore Colts use the Colts', Oakland and LA Raiders the Raiders', St. Louis Rams the Rams', and San Diego Chargers the Chargers'.
- Super Bowls XXIII, XXIX and XXXIII say MIAMI, while XLI, XLIV and LIV say MIAMI GARDENS. They were played at the same stadium; Miami Gardens only became a city in 2003.
- The plates and dates use the year the game was played (Super Bowl I, January 1967, is plate 67), not the year the season started.
